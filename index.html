Terms & FAQs

💬 Revision Policy

2 rounds of revisions included with every commission. Additional revisions are $15 per round.

⏰ Turnaround Time

Standard delivery is 5-12 business days depending on tier. Rush commissions available for +50% fee.

💰 Payment

50% deposit upfront via PayPal or Crypto. Remaining 50% upon completion. Custom rates available for large projects.

📁 File Formats

All commissions include: PSD (editable), PNG (transparent), and JPG formats. Wallpaper dimensions available in: 1920x1080, 2560x1440, 3840x2160, and mobile (1080x2160).

🚫 Commercial Use

Personal use only by default. Commercial licenses require separate negotiation. No reselling or redistribution without permission.

❓ Can I use your art as a Twitch/YouTube thumbnail?

Yes! Personal streaming use is always allowed. Please credit @ig_astaboyy in your description.

🔄 Refund Policy

Refunds available if work hasn't begun. 25% restocking fee applies after initial sketch. No refunds after line art stage.
